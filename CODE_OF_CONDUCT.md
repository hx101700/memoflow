# Code of Conduct

MemoFlow welcomes useful, respectful participation.

Participants should:

- discuss the work and its evidence rather than people;
- explain problems with enough detail for others to reproduce them;
- respect different levels of experience and language background;
- protect private recordings, credentials, and personal information.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate exposure of private data, and malicious submissions are not acceptable. Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when needed to keep the project safe and constructive.

For sensitive reports, contact the repository owner privately instead of posting credentials or private recordings in a public Issue.
